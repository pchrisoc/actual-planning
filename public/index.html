Single-filer mode subtracts traditional 401(k), eligible payroll HSA contributions, the standard deduction and eligible student loan interest before calculating federal liability. Health savings account (HSA)

The editable default is the 2027 self-only limit. The 2027 family limit is $9,000. Use your eligible limit and include employer deposits. This forecast assumes eligible pre-tax payroll contributions and estimates the federal income tax effect. See IRS HSA eligibility and tax rules.

With smart allocations off, a 100% election holds other planned contributions and loan payments until the priority source finishes. Multiple 100% elections run in the this order: 401(k), HSA, Roth IRA, loans, then savings goals. Taxes and required bills may still be due. Review cash shortfalls and loan due dates before using this scenario.

04

Your loans

Add each loan separately. A paycheck percentage uses average monthly gross wages and overrides the monthly dollar amount. Leave the payment blank to calculate the amount needed by its payoff date. Interest uses monthly APR/12 accrual. Mark eligible student loans to estimate the interest deduction. Enter APR before autopay discounts. For eligible federal autopay, enter a 1 percentage point total discount through June 30, 2028 and 0.25 afterward, subject to servicer confirmation. Optional refinancing uses your assumed final offered APR from its start date; federal consolidation itself uses a weighted-average rate.

05

Savings goals

Add any goal, such as an emergency fund, car, vacation or house deposit. Give each goal its own name, target and contributions. Enter current savings yourself; category allocations are not your savings balance. Contributions start on your first contribution / pay date above. Each goal can use its own frequency. A paycheck percentage uses average gross wages for that frequency and overrides the dollar contribution. Choose its Actual category below in Populate your Actual budget.

Smart allocations

Enter your required bills and use each loan’s monthly dollar override for its required payment. The planner reserves the full month’s bills, loan payments and cash buffer before distributing cash on paydays. Lower priority numbers fund first. In this mode the slider sets Roth IRA and savings deposits, which stop at their targets. The Roth IRA still follows its start date and deadline.

At 100% 401(k), the planner reserves modeled payroll taxes and other deductions. Optional deposits can start on the max-out payday if cash is available. Smart goal projections cover the selected year.

How to enable direct apply on your server

Edit /home/pchrisoc/financial-planner/.env on the Docker VM and set ACTUAL_WRITE_ENABLED=true. Then run:

cd /home/pchrisoc/financial-planner
docker compose up -d --no-deps planner

Refresh this page and populate a fresh preview. Enabling does not apply a draft automatically. All selected months must be readable, and applying still requires your review and confirmation. Local snapshot mode always stays read-only.

Each savings goal appears here automatically. Choose an existing Actual category for its contributions, or Do not populate to keep it as a forecast. If you need a new category, create it in Actual, then refresh categories here.

Income uses estimated take-home pay: scheduled salary less employee 401(k), payroll HSA contributions and estimated taxes under your selected tax method. Map Take-home pay after taxes and payroll contributions to an Actual income category. Employer contributions are not added to income. Forecast interest is not assigned to categories; investment-income tax is not modeled. Other categories are left alone.

Months to populate

When the start date is after the 1st, existing funded categories in that month stay unchanged. Later months use the forecast. Uncheck only to deliberately replace the whole start-month allocation.

401(k) and HSA payroll deductions and employer contributions are not assigned to spending categories. Income allocations use post-tax pay after payroll contributions, with a $0 minimum. Cash shortfalls stay visible in the forecast. IRA, loan and savings-goal contributions are budgeted as after-tax expenses. Forecast interest and tax on investment income are not assigned to categories. Other categories are left alone.
